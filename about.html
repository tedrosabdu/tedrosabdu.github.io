About

About Tedros Salih Abdu, researcher at SnT, University of Luxembourg, with experience in 5G/6G terrestrial and non-terrestrial networks, satellite communications, mathematical optimization, machine learning, and industrial R&D.

I am Tedros Salih Abdu, a researcher at the Interdisciplinary Centre for Security, Reliability and Trust (SnT), University of Luxembourg. My work focuses on the design and optimization of next-generation wireless communication systems, particularly terrestrial and non-terrestrial networks.

My background spans academic research, industrial R&D, and collaborative research-project leadership, with experience in mathematical optimization, machine learning, algorithm development, and system-level simulation for wireless and satellite communication systems.

Background

I received my Ph.D. in Computer Science from the University of Luxembourg in 2022, where my doctoral research focused on resource-management techniques for flexible broadband satellite communication systems.

I received a joint M.Sc. degree in Electrical Engineering from Pan African University and Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture and Technology, Kenya, in 2017, and a B.Sc. degree in Electronics and Communication Engineering from Mekelle University.

My professional experience spans both academia and industry. From 2017 to 2019, I was a Lecturer at Wollo University. I subsequently joined the University of Luxembourg, where I worked as a Doctoral Researcher and later as a Research Associate.

I also worked at Nokia as a Senior Algorithm and Simulation Engineer, contributing to algorithm development and system-level simulation for 5G systems. I later returned to SnT, University of Luxembourg, where I currently work on research and development for next-generation terrestrial and non-terrestrial communication systems.

Research Interests

My research interests include:

  • Terrestrial and non-terrestrial networks (TN/NTN)
  • Mobility and handover management
  • Radio-resource management
  • Satellite system planning and constellation optimization
  • Precoding, beamforming, and beam management
  • Channel modeling and channel estimation
  • Mathematical optimization for communication systems
  • Machine learning for wireless networks
  • Direct-to-Device satellite connectivity

My research combines communication-system modeling, mathematical optimization, machine learning, algorithm development, and system-level simulation.

Professional Service

Alongside my research, I contribute to the wireless-communications research community through conference and journal service.

I have served as a Technical Programme Committee (TPC) Member for the EuCNC & 6G Summit within the Wireless, Optical and Satellite Networks (WOS) track.

I have also reviewed research for international conferences including IEEE ICC, IEEE PIMRC, and EuCNC & 6G Summit, as well as journals including IEEE Transactions on Wireless Communications, IEEE Transactions on Aerospace and Electronic Systems, and IEEE Communications Letters.
