Publications

My research publications span terrestrial and non-terrestrial networks, mobility and handover, satellite system planning, radio-resource management, optimization, and AI/ML-assisted communication systems.

Selected publications are listed below. For my complete and most up-to-date publication record, please visit my Google Scholar profile.

Beam Placement & Resource Allocation

A Global Beam Placement and Resource Allocation Design for MEO-Constellation-Based Satellite Communication Systems

Vu Nguyen Ha, Tedros Salih Abdu, Ti Ti Nguyen, Hung Nguyen Kha, Eva Lagunas, Joel Grotz, and Symeon Chatzinotas
Research Square Preprint, 2026

A scalable framework for joint beam placement, bandwidth allocation, power allocation, and satellite-to-service-group association in large-scale MEO satellite systems. The work considers time-varying satellite visibility, heterogeneous user demand, payload-resource constraints, and spectrum- and energy-efficient operation.

Preprint

Preliminary work on joint beam placement and radio-resource allocation for large-scale MEO satellite constellations under non-uniform traffic demand.

Publication record

Doctoral Thesis

Resource Management Techniques for Flexible Broadband Satellite Communication Systems

Tedros Salih Abdu
Ph.D. Thesis, University of Luxembourg, 2022

My doctoral research investigated resource-management techniques for flexible broadband satellite communication systems, including power and bandwidth allocation, demand-aware resource management, interference management, and flexible satellite payload operation.

The doctoral research was supported by the Luxembourg National Research Fund (FNR) through the AFR programme under the project:

Power and Bandwidth Allocation for INterference-Limited SATellite Communication Systems
